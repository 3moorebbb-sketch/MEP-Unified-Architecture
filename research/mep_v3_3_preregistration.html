The Hypothesis

Catastrophic Forgetting can be mitigated without complex regularization penalties by using Complex-Valued Neural Networks (CVNNs). By mapping Task A to the Real axis ($\theta = 0$) and applying an Euler Phase Rotation operator ($\mathcal{P} = e^{i\pi/2}$) before learning Task B on the Imaginary axis, the tasks will occupy the same physical parameter space but remain mathematically orthogonal, resulting in zero or near-zero gradient interference.

1. Experimental Design

  • Benchmark: Split MNIST (Task A: Digits 0-4; Task B: Digits 5-9). This is identically matched to the V3.2 failure to allow for direct comparison.
  • Network: A fully Complex-Valued Multi-Layer Perceptron (ComplexLinear, ComplexReLU).
  • The Operation:
    1. Train on Task A (Inputs cast as pure Real numbers).
    2. Apply the literal MEP Master Equation operator: $W_{new} = W_{old} \times e^{i(\pi/2)}$. (Physically rotates the network 90 degrees).
    3. Train on Task B (Inputs cast as pure Imaginary numbers).

2. Baselines & Seeds

  • Naive Complex SGD: A CVNN trained on Task A and then Task B sequentially, without applying the Euler Phase Rotation. (Expected to suffer catastrophic forgetting).
  • V3.2 EWC Baseline: The real-valued Elastic Weight Consolidation baseline from the previous test (which achieved 4.99% retention).
  • Seeds: 20 independent random initializations.

3. Explicit Failure Conditions (Falsifiability)

Failure Conditions

The Complex Phase-Multiplexing hypothesis will be considered FAILED if:

  1. Phase Leakage: The MEP CVNN with Phase Rotation retains less than 85% relative accuracy on Task A. (If the non-linear activation functions cause the imaginary gradients to leak into the real axis and destroy the memory, the architecture is not viable).
  2. Baseline Equivalence: The MEP CVNN with Phase Rotation fails to outperform the "Naive Complex SGD" by a statistically significant margin ($p < 0.05$). If rotating the phase offers no benefit over doing nothing, the explicit geometric operator $\mathcal{P}$ is proven useless.
